# Protocol issues, run_A (13 Nov 2026)

Each entry: step id · what was unclear · what I did.

## Setup and definition

1. **S1 · composition given as ranges.** The only hemp block EPD (Schönthaler, Bau EPD) gives shives 20–55 % and binder 45–80 %. The Sonae Arauco OSB EPD also gives ranges. The protocol does not say how to turn a range into a share. **Did:** used midpoints, and normalised to 1 where the midpoints did not sum to 100 %. For hemp blocks this one choice decides the binder mass (0.45–0.80 kg) and so most of the GWP. Suggest a rule (midpoint? or the producer's datasheet as a tie-breaker?).
2. **S1 · ÖKOBAUDAT dataset with no composition table.** The ÖKOBAUDAT generic "Expandierter Kork" has no composition, and the Thünen OSB average puts it in an Excel annex. **Did:** went on to the EPD registries (EPD Hub HUB-0281 for cork). For OSB I used the 3 EPDs with a composition table and left out Thünen. The rule "median if several" does not say what to do when only some EPDs give a composition.
3. **S1 · "moisture counts with the constituent it is in" vs dataset moisture.** The hemp EPD counts block moisture (about 17 % of mass: 335 kg/m³ dry vs 405 kg/m³ at equilibrium) inside the shives share. The ecoinvent hemp hurd has 2 % water, so 0.375 kg of hurd carries the block's water as if it were hurd. The targeted check item 1 only covers the opposite error (water counted twice). **Did:** kept it as the protocol says, and noted it.
4. **S2 · packaging and storage steps.** EPD process lists end with palletising, stacking and packaging. **Did:** did not treat these as transformation steps (no rows). Worth stating explicitly.

## A1–A3 dataset rules

5. **D1 · "same constituents" for a constituent in a less finished state.** For cork, "cork, raw" is the same constituent, so D1 is yes for the constituent and the finishing steps come from D5. That reading worked, but the wording of D1 ("product … in its delivered state") and D5 ("When D1 finds a constituent only in a less finished state") overlap. Suggest that D1 for a constituent explicitly allows a less finished state.
6. **D2 · market for a processing service, GLO only.** For processing proxies (plaster mixing, wood chipping, compression of SMC, drying of grass), the only market is GLO, while production datasets exist for RER, CH or FR. Read literally, D2 (market first) gives GLO. D3 forbids only "global market groups", not "market for … GLO". **Did:** used the GLO markets (GeR 3). A rule such as "D3 over market and production datasets together" would give RER/CH production instead, which changes GeR and the electricity mixes.
7. **D2 · waste-treatment rows.** "market for waste wood, untreated" (CH, DE, FR) exists, but it mixes routes, while C3–C4 needs one row per route. **Did:** used the route-specific treatment datasets (no market exists for them). Suggest stating that C3–C4 rows skip D2.
8. **D3 · countries not on the list (PT).** Raw cork exists only as PT and RoW. Step 4 lists DE, FR, IT, ES, NL, AT, SE, FI, PL, CH; PT is a European country but not on the list, so read literally it falls under "7 any other", after RoW. **Did:** took PT under step 4 (a single European country, after the listed ones) and logged it. Suggest adding PT (and the other EU/EFTA countries) to the list or saying "any other European country" before GLO.
9. **D3 · CH vs GLO for waste treatment.** Waste wood incineration exists as GLO and as CH "municipal incineration FAE". The country-before-GLO rule picks the Swiss FAE variant. **Did:** followed the rule (CH FAE). Note that FAE (fly ash extraction) is a different technology variant, picked only because of geography.
10. **D4 · lorry size class.** The protocol says "lorry" with no size. I read this as "transport, freight, lorry, diesel, unspecified" (exact, D1). If D4 were applied to all lorry size and EURO-class markets, "first A–Z" would pick "16-32 metric ton, EURO 3", which is clearly unintended. Suggest naming the lorry dataset.
11. **D5 · no processing-only dataset exists.** Pressing hemp-lime in a block machine, cork autoclaving and cutting have no ecoinvent dataset that excludes the material. Most "same operation" candidates (kiln/air drying, sawing, planing, concrete blocks, AAC, expanded perlite, pellets, baling) contain the material itself or other materials. The protocol assumes "processing datasets … usually exclude the material", but often that is not true. **Did:**
    - Initial choice for pressing (hemp) and autoclave (cork): "compression of sheet moulding compound". It is the only processing-only "press in a mould" dataset, but it carries nylon, PE, PET, PP, film, steel castings and SMC scrap incineration (about 1.0–1.6 kg CO₂-eq/kg).
    - The targeted check (item 4, extra ingredients) replaced it: hemp pressing → plaster mixing (electricity and machine only); cork autoclave → "drying of grass" (heat, chopping, pressing, cooling of plant material).
    - D5 should say that a proxy must not carry other materials or consumables. Then this would have been caught at D5, not at STEP 3. Honest note: the replacements were chosen after I had seen the failing STEP 3 result. The justification is item 4 only, but a second run should check whether it reaches the same replacements without that knowledge.
12. **D5 · steps with no burden (ambient air drying).** The only air-drying datasets (sawnwood "air drying to u=20%") contain the wood and nothing else, so the operation itself has zero burden in ecoinvent. **Did:** no row for hemp block air drying, logged as "no row". The protocol says every step gets a row; suggest allowing "no row" when the closest dataset shows the step has no own inputs.
13. **D5 · proxy unit not per kg processed.** "drying of grass" is per litre (kg) of water evaporated. **Did:** took 1 litre per kg cork processed (assumption, no source for the steam use of cork autoclaving). This sets 0.55 of the 0.64 kg CO₂-eq/kg of cork board A1–A3. A source-based amount (steam or heat per kg ICB, e.g. from an Amorim LCA background report) should replace it.
14. **ROW · TiR not readable in Brightway.** The bw2io import keeps no time period (start/end) for ecoinvent datasets; some keep a free-text "Time period:" comment. **Did:** TiR from the comment where it gives years (end year), otherwise "unclear" → 5 (conservative). That pushes most rows' DQR up and makes cork and hempcrete A1–A3 DQR > 3 (low quality) because of TiR alone. TiR should be read from the ecospold time period, or the rule should define a default for undocumented ecoinvent datasets.
15. **ROW · DQR weighting with processing rows.** "mass-weighted over the rows": processing rows are per kg processed, not a mass of material. **Did:** weighted processing rows by their kg processed (1 kg each).
16. **ROW / rounding · 3 s.f. vs the A5 example.** 3 significant figures turns 1.0526 into 1.05 (the loss becomes 5.0 % instead of 5.26 %), while the protocol example writes 1.053. **Did:** wrote 1 + loss with the loss rounded to 3 s.f. (1.0526, 1.0309). All other amounts are rounded to 3 s.f.
17. **row_purpose for a D1-yes finished product.** The fixed names include `constituent: <name>` and `processing: <step>`, but none for a finished product taken whole. **Did:** `constituent: OSB/3`.

## STEP 3

18. **STEP 3 · one producer = one EPD with two datasets.** Hemp blocks: two ÖKOBAUDAT datasets from the same EPD/producer (405 and 430 kg/m³). **Did:** counted 2 datasets, median of both. Cork: only one generic (Sphera) dataset, which is a different product (80 kg/m³ expanded cork granulate/board, unspecified) from the 115 kg/m³ ICB. The protocol does not say whether generic datasets count as "EPDs".
19. **STEP 3 · density for per-kg conversion.** The Egger dataset's flow is named "607 kg/m³" but the EPD declares 611 kg/m³. **Did:** used the EPD's declared density (611). Small effect (0.7 %).

## A4 to C4

20. **A4 · T2 · evidence standard.** "Look for a manufacturer in each country" does not say how current the evidence must be. For OSB, IT production was found only in 2012–13 reports (I-Pan, Coniolo, plant under construction), and ES only as a planned line. **Did:** treated undated or old evidence as not confirmed → T2 unclear → conservative "no" → EU (1,500 km + 100 km sea). This alone adds 0.23 kg CO₂-eq/kg to OSB (A4 = 34 % of its cradle-to-grave GWP-fossil). A second run that accepts the 2013 report will get "national, 150 km". This is the most likely difference between runs; suggest requiring a source from the last 3 years, or an EPD / association member list.
21. **A4 · T2 · which branch is "conservative".** I took "no" (longer transport) as conservative. The protocol does not define the direction of "conservative" per test.
22. **A5 · I1 / C1 · X1 · "typical piece".** OSB boards range from 6 to 40 mm and 590 × 1800 to 2800 × 11500 mm (up to ~790 kg). **Did:** used the standard format 2500 × 1250 × 18 mm (SINTEF certificate) for "standard thickness" → 34 kg. A typical piece is not defined.
23. **C1 · X1 · fixing method not in the inputs.** Cork boards can be screwed (EPD A5) or bonded with adhesive (ETICS). **Did:** followed the EPD's installation description (screwed → no). The answer flips with the application, which the protocol keeps out of the inputs.
24. **C2 · E1 · product-type matches pointing to different categories.** Hemp-lime blocks are masonry (named under NMD 8, 32, 40) and insulation (named under 52, different route). Cork board is insulation (52) and a board (55). **Did:** hemp → "unclear" → No branch → heaviest constituent (lime binder) → no lime category → same main raw material → 40 (8 and 76 have the same shares, so the number differs between runs but the route does not); cork → 52. Suggest: match on the material first, product type only if the material is not named.
25. **C3–C4 · recycling dataset that is not a waste treatment.** For wood recycling the only dataset is "treatment of waste wood, post-consumer, sorting and shredding", whose reference product is wood chips (dry mass). **Did:** a positive amount, converted to dry mass (× 581.7/607). The template rule "waste sent to treatment has a negative amount" does not cover it.
26. **C3–C4 · no treatment datasets for hemp-lime or cork.** **Did:** proxies (waste concrete, not reinforced, for hemp-lime; untreated waste wood for cork), TeR 4. The protocol gives no D5-like proxy rule for treatment rows ("same rules D1 to D4").
27. **NMD revised scenarios (Nov 2026).** The protocol mentions revised NMD scenarios not yet read. **Did:** used the May 2024 appendix (the link in the protocol).
28. **END · output location.** END says save to `data/baseline_recipes/` under git; the prompt says `runs/run_A/`. **Did:** followed the prompt.
